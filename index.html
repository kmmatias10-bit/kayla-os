♡ About this application

Created and operated by Kayla Matias

Kayla OS (kOS) is a private, single-user household and meal-planning application. Its planned Pinterest connection will use read-only access to a board selected by the account owner to organize recipe ideas, compare them with pantry inventory, suggest weekly meals, and identify missing grocery ingredients.

kOS will not create, edit, or delete Pinterest content, and Pinterest information will not be sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for marketing.
