Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kayla OS (also called “kOS”) handles information when you use the application, including its optional Pinterest recipe-board connection.

Effective August 2, 2026

1. Information kOS handles

kOS is a personal household and meal-planning application. Depending on the features you use, it may handle information you enter, such as pantry items, recipes, grocery lists, meal plans, household tasks, calendar events, notes, preferences, and receipt details.

If you choose to connect Pinterest, kOS may receive information permitted by your authorization, including:

  • Your Pinterest account’s basic identification information needed to maintain the connection.
  • The boards you authorize kOS to access.
  • Pins from a board you select, including Pin titles, descriptions, images, links, and related metadata available through Pinterest.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide and improve kOS features. This may include organizing saved recipe ideas, comparing recipes with pantry inventory, recommending meals, creating meal plans and grocery lists, showing household reminders, and maintaining the preferences you choose.

Pinterest information is used only for the personal recipe and meal-planning functions you request.

3. Storage and security

Many kOS features store information locally in your web browser on your device. Information saved only in browser storage may be removed if you clear browser data or use kOS on a different device.

When the Pinterest connection is enabled, authorization credentials and synchronized information may be processed through secure services necessary to operate the connection. Reasonable safeguards will be used to protect this information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing and selling

kOS does not sell your personal information or Pinterest information. kOS does not use Pinterest information for advertising and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate kOS, maintain secure hosting, or provide a feature you request. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and application security.

5. Pinterest choices and deletion

Connecting Pinterest is optional. You may disconnect kOS through the application when that feature is available or revoke access through your Pinterest account settings. Revoking access stops future retrieval of Pinterest data but may not automatically delete recipe information you previously chose to save in kOS.

You may delete imported recipes and other saved information within kOS. You may also clear locally stored kOS data through the application’s Settings page or your browser controls.

kOS requests access only to support the Pinterest features you choose. It does not post, edit, or delete your Pins unless a future feature clearly asks for that permission and you authorize it.

6. Data retention

Information is kept only as long as needed to provide the features you use, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the application. Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, clear browser storage, or reset kOS.

7. Children’s privacy

kOS is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Pinterest connection.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as kOS features change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material updates are made.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about this Privacy Policy may be submitted to the owner of Kayla OS through the contact information associated with the Kayla OS Pinterest developer application.
